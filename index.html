ABOUT US

A local team that understands dogs

A Dog's Intelligence is a local dog care business serving Palmerston North and surrounding areas. We provide high-quality grooming, behaviour training, daytime dog care and holiday boarding services.

Our team loves dogs and focuses on safety, comfort and positive dog experiences. Local dog owners can trust us to look after their pets while they work or travel.
